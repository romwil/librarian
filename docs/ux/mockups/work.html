Piranesi

Susanna Clarke · 2020 · English

Description

Piranesi’s house is no ordinary building: its rooms are infinite, its corridors endless, its walls lined with thousands upon thousands of statues. Within the labyrinth of halls an ocean is imprisoned; waves thunder up staircases, rooms are flooded in an instant. But Piranesi is not lost; he understands the tides as surely as he understands the pattern of the labyrinth itself. He even lives to serve the One Other Inhabitant, his friend, the Other, whose motives for being in the House are far more inscrutable.
